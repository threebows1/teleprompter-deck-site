PDF Tools: Privacy

LAST UPDATED 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Your files stay on your device

PDF Tools at apps.mysecondwish.com/pdf-editor runs entirely inside your web browser. When you choose a PDF or an image, it is read and changed by code running on your own computer or phone. Your files, their contents and the passwords you type are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What is kept in your browser

Some things are saved in your browser's own storage on your device, so they are there next time. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • My documents. Files you add in the Workspace, and copies of files you finish in any tool, are kept in this browser (up to 200 MB; the oldest go first). You can delete any of them in Workspace → My documents, or turn this off with “Keep finished files here”.
  • Signatures you make in Sign PDF or Edit PDF, so you can use them again. Delete one with the × next to it.
  • Favourite tools you star.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it. We do not use cookies, accounts, analytics or ads on PDF Tools.

Things loaded from other sites

When you open the Type tab of the signature maker, the handwriting fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google receives the request for the font files, as with any website that uses them, but not your name or your documents: the signature itself is drawn on your device.
