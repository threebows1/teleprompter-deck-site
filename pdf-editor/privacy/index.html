PDF Tools: Privacy

LAST UPDATED 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Your files stay on your device

PDF Tools at apps.mysecondwish.com/pdf-editor runs entirely inside your web browser. When you choose a PDF or an image, it is read and changed by code running on your own computer or phone. Your files, their contents and the passwords you type are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What is kept in your browser

Some things are saved in your browser's own storage on your device, so they are there next time. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • My documents. Files you add in the Workspace, and copies of files you finish in any tool, are kept in this browser (up to 200 MB; the oldest go first). You can delete any of them in Workspace → My documents, or turn this off with “Keep finished files here”.
  • Signatures you make in Sign PDF or Edit PDF, so you can use them again. Delete one with the × next to it.
  • Favourite tools you star.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it. We do not use cookies, accounts, analytics or ads on PDF Tools.

Things loaded from other sites

A few tools fetch data from another site. None of them sends your files or your text anywhere:

  • OCR PDF downloads a language model (about 0.4–3 MB per language) from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time you use a language, and your browser keeps it for next time. This is the reading engine's data, not your file; the reading itself happens on your device.
  • HTML to PDF: if you type a web address, your browser asks that website for the page, the same as opening it in a tab.
  • Sign PDF: when you open the Type tab of the signature maker, the handwriting fonts load from Google Fonts. Google receives the request for the font files, not your name or your documents.

AI, camera and saved workflows

  • AI Summarizer and Translate PDF use the AI built into Google Chrome, which runs on your computer. Chrome may download its AI model the first time; your text is not sent to us or to Google for this. In other browsers the summarizer uses a simple method that also runs on your device, and Translate PDF is not available.
  • Scan to PDF uses your camera only while the camera view is open, with your permission. Photos stay on your device.
  • Workflows you save are kept in your browser. Passwords in them are never saved.
