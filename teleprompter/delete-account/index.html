Delete your data

Teleprompter Deck · com.mysecondwish.teleprompterdeck

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Cloud backup, tap Delete everything in the cloud.
  3. Confirm. Every backed-up recording is removed from the server straight away.

Scripts and recordings kept on the phone itself are removed by uninstalling the app, or by deleting them individually first. They were never sent anywhere.

By email

If the app is already uninstalled, write to mysecondwish11@gmail.com from the address on your Google Play account. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

When a subscription ends

Uploads stop the day it ends. Everything already backed up stays readable for 30 days, so you can download it without paying again or asking anyone. It is deleted 60 days after the subscription ends.

No account, no password

There is no sign-up and no login. Your backups are filed under a random id the app generates and hands to Google Play. We never see your name, your email or your Google account.
