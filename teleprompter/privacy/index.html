Privacy Policy — Teleprompter Deck

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Android app "Teleprompter Deck", package com.mysecondwish.teleprompterdeck.

The short version

Your scripts and recordings stay on your phone. Two optional features send something to our service when you use them — writing a draft with AI, and backing recordings up — and nothing else about what you write ever leaves the device. We count which features get used, never what you type into them.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored on your device only:

  • The scripts you write, paste or import, and the folders you put them in
  • Your settings — speed, text size, colors, typeface and the rest
  • A list of your recordings: which script each take came from, and how long it

ran

  • Whether you have an active subscription

Your video and audio takes are saved to your device's own gallery, in the same place as everything else your camera records.

Uninstalling the app removes the app's own data. Recordings already saved to your gallery stay there, because they are yours.

Permissions, and what each one is for

PermissionUsed for
CameraRecording video with the script on screen. Only while you are recording.
MicrophoneRecording audio, and voice-follow scrolling. Only while you are recording or in Voice mode.
Photos and videosSaving finished takes into your gallery, and listing them in the app afterwards.
Internet, network and Wi-Fi stateThe three optional features below, and nothing else.

The app works fully offline. If you never use the three features below, it never makes a network request.

When data leaves your device

1. Voice-follow scrolling. Speech recognition is performed by Android's own recognizer, not by us. Depending on your device and settings, Android may send audio to Google for processing. That is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one. If you prefer it never to happen, do not use Voice mode — every other scroll mode is entirely offline. Many Android devices can also be set to recognize speech on-device.

2. AI script generation. Optional, and part of a paid plan. When you use it, the topic, tone, length and audience you type are sent to our service, along with the proof of purchase the store gave your phone. Our service checks that proof with the store and then asks an AI provider to write the draft. We do not keep the topic after the draft comes back, and we do not use it to train anything. We never see your card details.

3. Subscriptions. Purchases are handled entirely by the app store. We never see your card details or your store account. The store tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

4. Backup, if you turn it on. Off by default. When you switch it on, the recordings and scripts you choose are uploaded to our service so you can restore them on another phone, together with the same proof of purchase. They stay there until you delete them, and you can delete them at any time from inside the app or by writing to us. Nobody else can read them, and we do not open them.

5. Analytics. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how its features are used — that the prompter was started, that a take was recorded, that the plans screen was opened. Firebase also records a device identifier, device model, app version, language, and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. It never records what you write: your scripts, your recordings and the topics you type for an AI draft are not sent to it.

Using a second phone as a remote is not on this list. Those two devices talk to each other directly over your own Wi-Fi network. Nothing passes through any server, ours or anyone else's.

What we collect about you

No advertising SDK, no account, and no sale of anything to anybody. We do not know what you have written or what is in your recordings.

We do see the usage figures Firebase Analytics reports, described above: which features get used, how often, and roughly where in the world from an IP address. We use them to decide what to build and fix, nothing else. Google holds that data under its own terms; you can read Firebase's privacy information at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you would rather not be counted, Android's own setting to opt out of ads personalisation limits what the app can report, and you can write to us to have anything associated with your device removed.

Google Play collects its own aggregate statistics about installs and crashes for any app on the store. We can see those aggregate figures in Play Console. They are not linked to you individually and we cannot use them to identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold nothing about you, there is nothing for us to show you, export or delete. Everything the app has is on your device and is deleted when you delete the app or the item.

To cancel a subscription, use Google Play's subscription settings — there is a link to it inside the app under Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Play.
