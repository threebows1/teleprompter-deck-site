Privacy policy

SIGNVEL

Last updated 27 October 2026. This policy covers the SignVel app for Android (package com.mysecondwish.signvel), made by Farrukh Shahzad. In short: your documents and signatures stay on your phone and are never uploaded by the app, the free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob (SignVel PRO removes it), and Google Play handles PRO purchases. SignVel has no accounts and collects no usage statistics.

Your documents and signatures

Everything SignVel does with your files happens on your phone: filling and signing, annotating, merging, reordering pages, adding bookmarks, password protection, digital certificate signatures, text extraction, converting images or text to PDF, and scanning. Farrukh Shahzad runs no server for the app, and SignVel never uploads your documents, signatures, scans or extracted text anywhere.

Documents you create or save, and the signatures you draw, type or import, are stored in SignVel’s private storage on your device. They leave your phone only when you choose to: for example when you tap Share and pick another app, print a document, or save a copy to your phone’s Photos. You can delete documents and signatures in the app at any time, and uninstalling SignVel deletes all of them.

When you sign with a digital certificate (.pfx or .p12), the certificate and its password are used on your phone only to create the signature. SignVel does not keep the password.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the app’s main screens, served by Google AdMob. SignVel PRO removes it. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, and may use it to personalise ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

SignVel PRO purchases

PRO is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; Farrukh Shahzad never sees them. The app asks Google Play whether this Google account owns PRO when it starts. That check goes only to Google Play. Google’s handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What else stays on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your device so they are there next time: the language you chose, which signature is your default, and whether you have seen the welcome screens. None of this leaves your phone.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: used only when you scan a document or a QR code or barcode. The images are processed on your phone by Google’s on-device scanning tools (ML Kit) and are not uploaded.
  • Photos and files: SignVel opens only the photos, PDFs or certificate files you pick yourself, for example to turn photos into a PDF or import a signature. On Android 12 and older it may ask for storage access to read and save files.
  • Internet: used for the ads in the free version, for checking and buying PRO through Google Play, and to open this website’s pages. Your documents are never sent over the internet by the app.
  • Advertising ID: used only by Google AdMob for the ads in the free version, as described above.

Sharing

Farrukh Shahzad does not sell your data and does not receive your documents. The free version shares the information described under “Ads in the free version” with Google AdMob for advertising, and PRO payments are handled by Google Play.

Children

SignVel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The free version shows ads.

Your choices

Because SignVel has no accounts and keeps your files only on your phone, there is nothing stored about you on our side to delete. To remove everything the app keeps, use Delete All Documents in the app’s documents screen and delete your signatures in My signatures, or simply uninstall the app.

This web page

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If the app’s handling of data changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the new version of the app is released.
