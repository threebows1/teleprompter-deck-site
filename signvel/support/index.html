Signing

How do I sign a document? Tap Fill & Sign on the home screen and open your PDF. Tap Auto Sign to place your default signature, or Add Sign to choose another. Drag it into place, resize it with the corner handle or by pinching with two fingers, then tap Done and Export.

How do I create a signature? Tap Create sign or the + button. Draw it with your finger, type your name in a handwriting style, or upload a photo of your signature. You can save several and pick one as your default in My signatures.

Is a SignVel signature legally valid? Electronic signatures are accepted for many everyday documents in many countries, but the rules depend on where you are and on who receives the document. When it matters, check what they require. For a cryptographic signature, use Protect › Digital Signature with your own .pfx or .p12 certificate.

My certificate gives “Signing Failed”. Check the password first. Some certificates exported recently from Windows or OpenSSL 3 use a newer encryption the PDF engine can’t read yet. Exporting the certificate again with “TripleDES-SHA1” (legacy) encryption usually fixes it.

Forms, scanning and tools

How do I fill a PDF form? Tap Forms › Fill PDF Form, open the form and tap a field to type, tick or choose. Save it as a fillable form, or flatten it so the answers can no longer be changed.

How do I scan paper? Tap Scan › Camera Scanner. The scanner finds the page edges and crops it for you; add more pages and save them as one PDF.

Extract text says “No Text Found”. The PDF is made of images, for example a scan, so it has no text to copy. Extract works on PDFs that contain real text.

Why are stamps and form labels in English when the app is in another language? Text written into the PDF itself uses the standard PDF fonts, which can’t show every script, so it stays in English. The app’s own screens follow the language you choose.

Your files

Where are my documents? In the Documents tab. Everything is kept on your phone in SignVel’s own storage; nothing is uploaded. Tap a document to open it, then use the share button to send it. The ⋮ menu deletes it, and Print on the home screen prints it.

Will I lose my files if I uninstall? Yes. Files kept only inside SignVel are deleted with the app, so share or save a copy of anything important first.

How do I change the language? Settings › Language. SignVel is available in English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

SignVel PRO

What does PRO do? It removes the ads.

I bought PRO but still see ads. Make sure the Play Store is signed in to the Google account you bought it with, then open Settings in SignVel and tap Restore purchases. Some payment methods stay “pending” for a while.

How do I cancel? Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose SignVel and tap Cancel subscription. PRO stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play within its refund window (see Google Play refund policy). If that doesn’t work for you, email us and we’ll look at it.
