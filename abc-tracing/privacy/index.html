Privacy Policy

ABC Letter Tracing 123 · Android version 1.1 · Updated 4 November 2026

MySecondWish provides this collection of early-learning games. Contact: mysecondwish11@gmail.com.

Games and local data

Games, drawings and tracing work offline. The speaker preference and parent choices are stored on the device. Drawings and tracing input are cleared when leaving an activity and are never uploaded. No account, name, email, location, camera, microphone or contacts are requested by the game.

Advertising

Google AdMob displays a banner only inside the protected parent area. We configure child-directed, under-age-of-consent, general-audience and non-personalised ad requests before advertising starts. Android advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions are removed. AdMob may process IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device/app information and ad interactions to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Ads require Internet access. No ad is shown over a learning activity.

Optional Firebase usage and crash reporting

Reporting is off until enabled in parent settings. If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics receive app usage events, device/app information, installation identifiers, crash logs and diagnostics. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalisation are disabled. Names, drawings and tracing strokes are never included.

Turning reporting off stops future collection, resets local analytics data and deletes unsent crash reports; it does not delete reports already sent. Contact us about information already submitted. Google processes these reports under its service retention practices.

Parent controls and choices

An arithmetic question protects parent settings. You may use the games without opening the parent area or enabling optional reports. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove locally saved settings. Your operating system and Google Play have separate privacy practices.
