Privacy Policy

ABC Letter Tracing 123 (Android) · Developer: MySecondWish · Effective date: 3 November 2026

In short: ABC Letter Tracing 123 is a learning app for young children. Children do not create accounts and are never asked for their name, email, photos, location or any other personal information. The app shows only child-appropriate, non-personalised ads, and uses anonymous crash and usage statistics to keep the app working well.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how MySecondWish ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the ABC Letter Tracing 123 app (the "App"), available on Google Play. If you have any questions, contact us at mysecondwish11@gmail.com.

2. Designed for children

The App is directed at children and is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no sign-up, no chat, no social features, and no links that let children leave the App without a parental gate.

3. Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings or contacts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Account logins or passwords (the App has no accounts)

Tracing progress and settings are stored only on the device and are deleted when the App is uninstalled.

4. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The App uses the following Google services. They may collect limited technical, non-identifying information as described below.

ServicePurposeData it may process
Google AdMob Showing ads that keep the App free Device type, OS version, app version, IP address (used transiently, not stored by us), ad interaction events. Ad requests are tagged as child-directed, so ads are non-personalised, are limited to family-friendly content, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
Firebase Crashlytics Finding and fixing crashes Crash logs, device model, OS version, app version, a random installation identifier.
Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) Understanding which screens and letters are used, to improve the App Anonymous app usage events (e.g. screen opened, lesson completed), device model, OS version, app version, general country. Advertising ID collection is disabled.

We do not sell any data, and we do not share data with third parties other than these service providers, which process it on our behalf. You can read their policies here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How AdMob uses data
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

5. Ads and parental gate

Ads shown in the App are selected to be appropriate for children. Any button that leads outside the App (for example to Google Play or our website) is protected by a parental gate that a young child cannot easily pass.

6. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed to work and to show ads, such as Internet access and network state. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location permissions.

7. Data retention and security

Crash and analytics data are kept by Google according to the retention settings of Firebase (we keep analytics data for the shortest available period). Data is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is usually nothing linked to your child that we can identify. Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on the device. If you believe information about your child has been collected, or you want to ask about access or deletion, email mysecondwish11@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
