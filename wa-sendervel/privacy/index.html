Privacy policy

WA SENDERVEL

Last updated 23 October 2026. This policy covers the Wa SenderVel extension for Google Chrome, made by Farrukh Shahzad. Wa SenderVel works inside WhatsApp Web and sends personalised messages to a list of your contacts. In short: the developer collects nothing, and your contacts, messages and reports stay in your browser.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Wa SenderVel has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no servers. It does not send any information to the developer or to any third party.

Cost

Wa SenderVel is free. There is nothing to buy, and no payment details are ever asked for.

What is stored in your browser

The extension saves these items with Chrome's local extension storage, on this computer only:

  • your settings: sending pace and schedule, sending mode, daily limit, privacy options and where you placed the header button;
  • your draft campaign: the phone numbers you paste or the CSV file you import (with all its columns), your message versions, and the file you choose to attach;
  • the current or last campaign: each phone number, the message it was given, its result (sent, not on WhatsApp, blocked, skipped or failed) and the time;
  • counts of how many messages were sent today and this month, for the daily limit.

None of this is synced or uploaded. You can delete the campaign report from the extension's Settings, and removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

How it works with WhatsApp

Wa SenderVel only runs on web.whatsapp.com. For each recipient it opens the chat through WhatsApp Web's own message link, puts your message in the message box, and either presses Send or waits for you to press it. Your messages are therefore sent by your own WhatsApp account, like any message you type yourself, and WhatsApp handles them under its own terms and privacy policy.

To do its job, the extension reads parts of the WhatsApp Web page while it is open: whether a chat opened, whether a number is not on WhatsApp, and whether a message has finished sending. For the privacy options, it marks contact names, photos and message text in the page so they can be blurred on your screen. This happens in your browser. Nothing it reads is stored, except the result of each send listed above, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Reports

When you download a campaign report, it is created in your browser and saved straight to your downloads folder. It is never uploaded.

Your contacts

You choose who receives your messages. Only message people who have agreed to hear from you, and follow WhatsApp's Terms of Service and the data protection laws that apply to you.
