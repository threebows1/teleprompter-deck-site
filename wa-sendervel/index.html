Wa SenderVel

YOUR CUSTOMERS. ONE CAMPAIGN.

A Chrome extension that sends personalised messages to your customer list from WhatsApp Web. Paste numbers or import a CSV, write up to five versions of your message, choose a pace, and follow every send with a live list and a downloadable report.

Your contacts and messages stay in your browser. There are no servers and no accounts.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.
